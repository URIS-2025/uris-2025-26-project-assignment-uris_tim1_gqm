What is GQM+?

Goal-Question-Metric (GQM+) is a framework for aligning organizational strategy with measurable outcomes.

Goals are refined into questions, which are evaluated using measurable targets and analytics.

Defining Goals with GQM Methodology

The GQM (Goal-Question-Metric) approach is a top-down framework used to drive meaningful data collection by ensuring every metric is directly tied to a business objective. The process begins by defining a Goal (the conceptual level), followed by generating Questions (the operational level) that characterize the goal, and finally identifying specific Metrics (the quantitative level) to provide the answers. To bridge the gap between intent and execution, a Strategy is often included to define the specific approach used to achieve the goal.

Example: Enhancing Customer Service Efficiency
Strategic Goal: Improve the quality and speed of the customer support process to increase long-term user retention.
Strategy: Implement a "First-Touch Resolution" initiative by empowering agents with an expanded internal knowledge base and advanced troubleshooting tools.
GQM Goal: Purpose: Improve | Object: Customer Support Process | Viewpoint: Support Management | Quality Attribute: Operational Efficiency and Satisfaction.
Question 1: How effectively are we resolving customer issues during the very first interaction?
